AeolusSurfaceMap.jpg
====================

Equirectangular Jupiter texture by Solar System Scope, based on NASA imagery.
Source: http://solarsystemscope.com/textures
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Derived work for the fictional gas giant Aeolus:
  * the Great Red Spot and its white collar were removed by inpainting;
  * the latitude geometry was remapped to change the band widths;
  * the palette was changed to violet belts and pale cyan zones.

The shader adds its own lighting, limb haze and cloud relief on top of the map.
