## Method notes

* **K1–K4**: oracle = closed-form Kepler equation (Newton on eccentric anomaly),
  audited against `KeplerUniversal` (universal variables, Laguerre/Conway).
* **K5–K6**: oracle = `KeplerUniversal` (TASK.md table), audited against Barker's
  equation (parabola) and the hyperbolic Kepler equation.
* **K7–K8**: closed form; audited by the initial-condition match, a central-difference
  ODE residual (finite-difference limited, threshold 1e-6) and a DOP853 rtol 1e-13
  cross-check (threshold 1e-9). The floor is the double round-off of the formula.
* **T1, T3**: oracle = dd-Taylor (order 32, tol 1e-30); floor = |K32 − K36| at the
  final checkpoint. T1 is also certified by a fixed-step DOP853 5-level Richardson
  (order 8). T3 is chaotic: the DOP853 levels cannot converge pointwise, the
  disagreement is recorded as the chaos diagnostic and does not gate validity.
* **T2**: exact rigid rotation; audited by substituting the state into the Newtonian
  equations (residual |a_computed − (−ω²r)| / scale) and by the zero momentum/COM check.

A level A1/A2/A3 is evaluated for a problem only when `floor <= 0.1 * threshold`
(TASK.md section 7); otherwise the level is SKIP with reason `oracle floor`.
