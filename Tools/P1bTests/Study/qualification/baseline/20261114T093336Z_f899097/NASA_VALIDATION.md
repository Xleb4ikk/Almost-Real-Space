## Limitations (unchanged block, source: `Study/jpl/README.md` section 4)

1. Model structure (solar J2, the small-body set) was chosen after inspecting the
   2026 residuals; 2016 is a holdout for the parameter values, not for the structure.
2. The full EIH is worse than 1PN on the Moon and the inner planets; the reason is not
   understood. EIH with the 409 small bodies was not run.
3. `rms8_end` comes from a file sampled once per year; Mercury (88 d) and the Moon
   (27 d) are phase-sensitive at 365 d sampling.
4. The 409 small bodies are test particles: they feel the planets but do not perturb
   each other.
5. The satellites are limited by the model (thousands of km), not by the integrator.
6. This uses the study DOP853, not the production game integrator.
